Read Your Sheet

A Chrome extension that soft-blocks distracting sites while your Reading List is growing faster than you read it.

It is deliberately not a hard blocker. When you hit a blocked site it shows a nag screen with your actual numbers, makes you sit for a few seconds, offers you something from your own Reading List instead — and then lets you through if you still want to go. The point is friction, not a lock. Sometimes you genuinely need YouTube at work.
